Title: Operation Numidian
Difficulty: easy
Category: Forensics

Description:
Our Advanced Physical & Propulsion Studies laboratory experienced a severe data breach on June 17, 2026. An attacker successfully bypassed our firewall, compromised a development server, and exfiltrated a highly classified telemetry report regarding the "Numidian Core" prototype.

We have managed to capture the network traffic during the attack window. 

Your mission:
1. Analyze the network capture to identify the initial compromise vector and locate the exfiltrated data.
2. The attacker encrypted the stolen data before exfiltration. Recover the key and decrypt the payload.
3. The recovered document is heavily obfuscated using advanced font-level tampering. Reverse-engineer the obfuscation to recover the true telemetry token.

Assets provided:
- nit_breach_1_3.pcap
